Privacy Policy

Mortgage Calculator · Think Launcher · Last updated September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Think Launcher ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the Mortgage Calculator mobile app (the "App").

The short version

The App does not require an account, and does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on our servers. All loan details you enter (loan amount, interest rate, term, and any saved scenarios) stay on your device only. We never see them.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data. The App has no user accounts and no login.

Data stored on your device

Loan scenarios and settings you create in the App are stored locally on your device using standard device storage. This data is not transmitted to us, and is not backed up to any server we control. If you delete the App, this data is removed along with it.

Subscriptions and payment

If you choose to purchase a Premium subscription, that purchase is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive, process, or store your payment card details or billing information — Google does. Google's handling of that information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Third-party services

The only third-party service the App relies on is Google Play Billing, used solely to process subscription payments. The App does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children and is not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Data security

Because your loan data stays on your device and is never transmitted to us, its security is governed by your device's own security (screen lock, encryption, etc.), not by us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at thinklauncher@gmail.com.
