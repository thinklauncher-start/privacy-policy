Privacy Policy

Mortgage Payoff Planner · Think Launcher · Last updated October 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Think Launcher ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the Mortgage Payoff Planner mobile app (the "App"), formerly named Mortgage Calculator.

The short version

The App does not require an account, and does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on our servers. All loan details you enter (loan amount, interest rate, term, and any saved scenarios) stay on your device only. We never see them.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data. The App has no user accounts and no login.

Data stored on your device

Loan scenarios and settings you create in the App are stored locally on your device using standard device storage. This data is not transmitted to us, and is not backed up to any server we control. If you delete the App, this data is removed along with it. Your device's own backup service (such as iCloud or Google backup) may include this data, and that is controlled by Apple or Google, not by us.

Subscriptions and payment

If you choose to purchase a Premium subscription, the purchase is handled entirely by the store you use: Apple's App Store on iOS, or Google Play Billing on Android. We do not receive, process, or store your payment card details or billing information. Apple or Google does. Their handling of that information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

Free trials and renewals are managed by the store. You can cancel any time in your Apple Account settings (iOS) or in Google Play under Payments and subscriptions (Android).

Third-party services

The App relies on the payment systems of Apple's App Store and Google Play, used solely to process subscription payments. The App does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

The Scenarios screen displays a decorative photo loaded from Unsplash (unsplash.com). When it loads, your device connects to Unsplash, which may receive your IP address under its own privacy policy. We send no personal information or loan data.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children and is not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Data security

Because your loan data stays on your device and is never transmitted to us, its security is governed by your device's own security (screen lock, encryption, etc.), not by us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at thinklauncher@gmail.com.
